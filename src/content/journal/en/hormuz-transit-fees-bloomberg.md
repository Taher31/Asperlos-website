On 24 March 2026 (4 Farvardin 1405), Bloomberg cited unnamed people familiar with the matter as saying that some commercial ships had been asked to pay to pass through the Strait of Hormuz and that some had paid. The figure of up to **2 million US dollars per voyage** was an amount requested in some cases. The report did not identify a uniform fee for all vessels.

In a follow-up on 1 April (12 Farvardin), Bloomberg cited people familiar with the negotiations as saying that talks for some oil tankers started at around one US dollar per barrel. The report also mentioned Chinese yuan and stablecoins as payment methods. Both reports describe conditions at the time.

## How to read shipping reports

When comparing reports, keep three figures separate: the amount requested, the amount actually paid and the total shipping cost. A figure from a single negotiation cannot, by itself, establish what other voyages will cost.
